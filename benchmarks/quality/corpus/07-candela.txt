The candela, symbol cd, is the SI base unit of luminous intensity. Its definition fixes luminous efficacy at 683 lumens per watt for monochromatic radiation of frequency 540 × 10^12 hertz.

Provenance: BIPM, The International System of Units (SI Brochure), 9th edition, version 3.01 (2025), https://doi.org/10.59161/AUEZ1291
